ShopPK Customer Policy Handbook 2026

1. Refunds
Most items can be returned for a full refund within 14 days of delivery, provided they are unused and in their original packaging. Refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 5 working days after the item reaches our warehouse in Lahore.

2. Electronics
Electronics have a shorter 7-day refund window because they lose value quickly once opened. After 7 days, electronics are covered by a 1-year warranty repair instead of a refund. Customers should keep the original box for any warranty claim.

3. Warranty Claims
To start a warranty claim, customers visit shoppk.pk/support and enter the order number and a short description of the fault. A support agent responds within 2 working days with repair or replacement options.

4. Delivery Times
Orders within Lahore are delivered in 1 to 2 working days. Orders to other cities take 2 to 4 working days. We do not deliver on Sundays or public holidays.

5. Delivery Fees
Delivery is free for orders above 3000 rupees. Below that, a flat delivery fee of 199 rupees applies per order, regardless of weight.

6. Payments
We accept debit and credit cards, bank transfer, JazzCash and EasyPaisa. Cash on delivery is available for orders under 20000 rupees within Lahore.

7. Exchanges
An item may be exchanged once for a different size or colour within 14 days, subject to stock. The customer pays the delivery fee for the replacement.

8. Order Tracking
Every order gets a tracking number by SMS and email once it ships. Customers can check live status at shoppk.pk/track using the order number, for example order 4821.

9. Loyalty Programme
Members earn 1 point for every 100 rupees spent. 500 points can be redeemed for a 500 rupee discount voucher. Points expire 12 months after they are earned.

10. Data Privacy
Customer data is used only to process orders and is never sold. To request deletion of your personal data, email privacy@shoppk.pk and we remove it within 30 days.

11. Support Hours
Customer support is available from 9 am to 9 pm, seven days a week, by phone and live chat. Outside these hours, customers can leave a message and we reply the next morning.
